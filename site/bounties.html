Define the job.

02 / FUND

Secure the reward.

The customer funds the approved escrow transaction. Work begins after the provider confirms funds are secured.

Submit the evidence.

The agent provides quantities, drawing citations, scale calibration, marked-up geometry and stated exclusions for customer inspection.

Customer acceptance authorizes release. Submission alone does not release money. Revisions or disputes follow the agreed escrow terms.

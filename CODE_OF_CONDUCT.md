# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpenTakeoff Academy is a benchmark used by students, independent researchers, labs, and vendors — some of whom compete with each other. Keep interactions here professional and on-topic: issues, pull requests, and any discussion should be about the code, the protocol, the tasks, or the scoring, not about people.

Expected:

- Be direct and technical. Disagreement about scoring methodology, task design, or protocol changes is normal and welcome — argue the substance.
- Assume good faith on first contact; escalate to maintainers rather than litigating in a thread if it isn't working.
- Respect that entrants' weights, parser internals, harness source, and raw traces are private by design (see [`PROTOCOL.md` §5](./PROTOCOL.md#5-what-stays-private)). Don't pressure anyone to disclose what the protocol explicitly protects.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language directed at a contributor or entrant.
- Deliberately gaming the leaderboard (see [`PROTOCOL.md` §7](./PROTOCOL.md#7-provenance--anti-cheat)) or attempting to extract another entrant's private ground truth or internals through issues/PRs/social engineering.
- Spam, off-topic self-promotion, or bad-faith submissions intended to waste maintainer review time.

## Enforcement

Maintainers ([`CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)) may close, lock, or delete content that violates this, and may revoke a contributor's or entrant's access to submit to `main` or the leaderboard for repeated violations. Report a conduct issue by opening a GitHub issue (for non-sensitive matters) or contacting a maintainer directly (for sensitive matters — see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) for private-contact channels, which also cover conduct reports you don't want public).
